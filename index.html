COMMON PROBLEMS

📱

SOCIAL MEDIA TRAP

Endless scrolling steals your time and attention.

✓ SOLUTION:

Set time limits. Turn off notifications. Use apps only 2 times per day.

🌙

LATE NIGHT USE

Blue light before bed makes sleeping difficult.

✓ SOLUTION:

No phone 1 hour before sleep. Charge phone outside bedroom. Read a book instead.

♾️

ENDLESS SCROLLING

Apps are designed to keep you watching forever.

✓ SOLUTION:

Set a timer. Delete addictive apps. Choose quality content over quantity.

🔔

NOTIFICATION OVERLOAD

Every ping interrupts your focus and peace.

✓ SOLUTION:

Turn off all non-essential notifications. Check messages on YOUR time, not app's time.

5 GOLDEN RULES

01

NO PHONE IN BEDROOM

Your bedroom is for sleeping, not scrolling.

Leave your phone in another room. Use a real alarm clock.

Better sleep = Better life

02

TECH-FREE MORNINGS

Don't check your phone for the first hour after waking up.

Start your day with YOU, not with other people's posts.

Your morning sets your mood

03

SET CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Decide when and where you use your phone.

Examples: No phone while eating. No phone when talking to people.

Be present in real life

04

FIND REAL HOBBIES

Replace screen time with activities you enjoy.

Reading, sports, music, cooking, walking in nature.

Real experiences > Virtual ones

05

WEEKLY DIGITAL DETOX

One day per week with minimal phone use.

Turn off social media. Use phone only for calls and messages.

Reconnect with yourself

[04]

IT SECURITY CHECKLIST

🔐 STRONG PASSWORDS

Use different passwords for different accounts.

Make passwords long (at least 12 characters).

Mix letters, numbers, and symbols: My$ecur3Pass2024!

TIP: Use a password manager app to remember them all.

🛡️ PROTECT PRIVATE INFO

Never share: passwords, bank details, ID numbers online.

Be careful what you post on social media.

Check privacy settings on all apps.

REMEMBER: Once online, it stays online forever.

⚠️ AVOID SUSPICIOUS LINKS

Don't click links in strange emails or messages.

Check the sender's email address carefully.

If something looks too good to be true, it probably is.

PHISHING: Fake messages trying to steal your information.

✅ SECURE YOUR ACCOUNTS

Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) everywhere.

Log out from public computers.

Update your apps and operating system regularly.

2FA: Second security check (code to your phone) when logging in.
